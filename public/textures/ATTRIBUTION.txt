IMPORTANT SOURCE NOTES
Solar System Scope describes these as equirectangular planetary maps based on NASA elevation and imagery data, with tuned colors and some fictional terrain used to fill unmapped areas. They are visual textures, not primary scientific datasets and must not be presented as scientific measurements.
Indicate that the maps were adapted when redistributing modified derivatives. Preserve the CC BY 4.0 attribution and link the licence.
