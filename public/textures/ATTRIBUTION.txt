SILENT FLEET — Moon and Mars textures
=====================================

Source maps:
  Solar System Scope / INOVE planetary texture pack
  https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations in this build:
  The provided 8K Moon and Mars maps are retained. The runtime uses 4K desktop
  and 2K mobile level-of-detail copies. Height/bump maps were generated from the
  source-map luminance for displacement and surface shading. These are adaptations
  of the CC BY 4.0 maps.

  Moon maps use a Mean Earth / Polar Axis equirectangular frame.
  Mars maps use a planetocentric east-longitude equirectangular frame.

Solar System Scope states that its texture pack is based on NASA elevation and
imagery data, with tuned colors; unmapped regions may contain fictional terrain.
The textures are credited to Solar System Scope / INOVE, not represented as raw
NASA imagery. NASA CGI Moon Kit (SVS 4720) is a related NASA visualization
reference, not the source of these texture files:
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
